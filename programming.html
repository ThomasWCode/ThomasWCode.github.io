My longest-running hobby

Programming

I started coding at seven and have been making games, simulations, apps and websites ever since.

Visit my GitHubOld YouTube channel

How it started

From Lua to full apps.

My first language was Lua. I quickly moved on to Python, which remains my favourite and strongest language. I also work with JavaScript, HTML and CSS, and have briefly explored C++.

Over the years, I have built physics simulations, 2D and 3D games, websites, an Android app and Windows software. One of my earliest projects was a YouTube channel teaching Roblox Studio and Lua.

I also created a website for the Friends of St John’s Garden using Squarespace, including custom embedded code. Visit the garden website.

The recurring themes

  • Turning an idea into something you can actually use
  • Experimenting with physics and game systems
  • Making complicated tools simple for other people
