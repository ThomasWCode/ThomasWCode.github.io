About me

A bit about me

My main interests are programming (and other tech stuff), astrophysics, particle physics and hiking. I started programming when I was seven and it’s still the hobby I spend the most time on. I also enjoy Design & Technology, especially when it involves actually making something.

Running is my favourite sport. I also play drums, spent several years doing LAMDA and read quite a lot of science fiction. The Bobiverse books and Stephen Hawking’s writing are particular favourites.

I also have a cat called Dusty, who has his own website...
