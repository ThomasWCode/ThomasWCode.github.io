A quick introduction

Curious about quite a lot.

Programming has been my biggest hobby since I was seven, but it shares the stage with astrophysics, particle physics and Design & Technology. I like making things, understanding how they work and getting far too absorbed in the details.

Away from a screen, I’m usually running, hiking, playing drums or doing drama through LAMDA. Science-fiction books are another longstanding favourite—especially the Bobiverse series and Stephen Hawking’s writing.

I also have a cat called Dusty, who naturally has his own website.
