Bridging the Gap

My TEDx talk was about why General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics don’t get along—and a few of the ideas trying to fix that.

Watch the full talkView the TEDx event

The idea

Two successful theories that do not fit.

General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics are our two leading descriptions of the universe, yet they become incompatible in the extreme situations where both should matter.

In the talk, I introduced quantum gravity and some of the possibilities for resolving that conflict, including String Theory as a candidate for a unified “Theory of Everything”.

Researching and giving the talk meant building on what I already knew and then actually explaining it to a room full of people, which was a genuinely exciting thing to do. The event had eight youth speakers and four older speakers, all loosely tied to the theme “Bridges”—everyone interpreted that differently.
