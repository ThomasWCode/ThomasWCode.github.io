Volunteering

Community events, green spaces and a charity shop have given me plenty of chances to help out—and meet some excellent people along the way.

Local green spaces

Islington parks and events

I've made many friends within the Islington Council Parks team and have been lucky to help at events including the Islington Grow Show, Apple Day and the Spring Festival at Gillespie Park.

The work has ranged from welcoming visitors to making apple juice and cataloguing show entries. I've even appeared occasionally in the council newsletter, Islington Life and the Islington Gazette.

I also built a website for the Friends of St John’s Garden, helping a brilliant local group make more people aware of their small but valuable green space. Visit the garden website.

Why I keep going back

Mostly, it's the people

The best bit has always been getting to know the people who run and turn up to these events. It stops feeling like a chore once you actually know everyone.

A different kind of contribution

Mind charity shop

I also volunteered at my local Mind charity shop. It was a chance to contribute to a charity that supports many people while learning the practical rhythm of a shop.
