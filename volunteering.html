Things I’ve helped with

Volunteering

Much of my volunteering has been at local council events in Islington, plus some time at my local Mind charity shop. Unsurprisingly, I've also done lots online: mostly coding for charities and local groups, but also helping with social media and websites.

Around Islington

Parks and local events

I’ve helped the Islington Council Parks team at the Grow Show, Apple Day and the Spring Festival at Gillespie Park. Knowing everyone who runs these events makes it hard to get away!

At different events I welcomed visitors, made apple juice and catalogued show entries. I’ve also popped up in Islington Life and the Islington Gazette a few times.

I also made the Friends of St John’s Garden website for a local council group that looks after a small green space in Farringdon.
