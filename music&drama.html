I do have other hobbies!

Music & Drama

Actually, I do have hobbies other than programming! I play drums and I spent several years doing LAMDA.

Drums (and a bit of piano)

Music

I started learning piano and drums in primary school. Drums suited me much better, so those are what I stuck with. I passed Grades 1 to 5 with three merits and two distinctions.

I also played in a band with two school friends. We performed at several school concerts, including one where we played “Sunday Bloody Sunday”.

The best bit

Playing with other people

Most of all, I enjoy learning and playing songs. Playing in a band with friends is much more fun than practising on my own.

What the exams involved

Monologues and duologues

For each exam I learned and performed either a solo monologue or a scene with a partner. It was as much about understanding the character as remembering the words.

Drama

LAMDA

LAMDA is an awarding body for drama and performance. I passed Grades 1 through 6, including the Grade 6 Bronze Medal, with five distinctions and one merit.

It was very different from music or programming, which is exactly why I enjoyed it.
