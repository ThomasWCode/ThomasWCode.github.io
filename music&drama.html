Beyond the code

Music & Drama

Actually, I do have hobbies other than programming: mainly drums, live performance and LAMDA.

Keeping time

Music

I started learning piano and drums in primary school, but drums quickly proved much more suited to me. I worked through Grades 1 to 5, earning three merits and two distinctions.

The part I enjoy most is simply learning and playing tracks. I also performed in a band with two school friends, including multiple school concerts and a performance of “Sunday Bloody Sunday”.

  • Five drum grades
    Three merits and two distinctions
  • Live performance
    School concerts with friends

What I like about it

Playing together

Drumming is technical, but you’re also completely reliant on listening to everyone else around you. That mix is what keeps it interesting for me.

On stage

Solo and paired scenes

LAMDA performances are either a solo monologue or a scene with a partner—working out how to interpret it, then actually performing it, voice and movement and all.

Words and performance

LAMDA

LAMDA is an awarding body for drama and performance. I passed Grades 1 through 6, including the Grade 6 Bronze Medal, with five distinctions and one merit.

It's a very different challenge from music or programming, which is exactly why I enjoy it.
