Getting outside

Sport

Running is the favourite, but squash, tennis, football, obstacle races and triathlons have all found their way in.

A regular Saturday

Running

I enjoy running and often go to my local 5K parkrun with friends. It's the sport I return to most consistently: simple, sociable and always a challenge against my own previous time.

Very muddy

Spartan races

I've taken part in Spartan races with my dad and friends, progressing from a children’s 2.5K to a 5K and then a 10K.

The races combine distance running with walls, barbed-wire crawls, sandbags and dozens of other obstacles. They are difficult, muddy and extremely good fun.

If you haven’t heard of them, Spartan’s website has more, but honestly the mud and the barbed wire say most of it.

Swim, cycle, run

Brighton Triathlon

I've also taken on the Brighton Triathlon with the same group of friends. It's always great fun, although the sea swim can be just a tiny bit cold :)

Beyond running

Other sports

I played squash between eight and 13, mainly for fun but with a few tournaments along the way. Tennis is another favourite, particularly during the summer.

Football has never been my strongest sport. At school, I eventually chose cross-country instead—which probably says everything.
