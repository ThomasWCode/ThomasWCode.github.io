My music project

Eigenstate Blue

I write alternative-rock songs, record the drums and put the rest together on my laptop. The name comes from quantum mechanics.

The name

Why Eigenstate Blue?

Eigenstate Blue is the name I use for my music. In quantum mechanics, an eigenstate has one definite value. “Blue” is meant to be the opposite: something less definite and more emotional.

I write the lyrics and make the instrumental. My own singing is genuinely bad, so the vocals aren’t me.

How I make it

I normally start with the lyrics, then build the instrumental in GarageBand. I record the drums live and make the other parts on my laptop. The vocal track is made with AI software using my lyrics, instrumental and a lot of line-by-line direction.

The singing itself is AI-generated, which is why I call the tracks partially AI-generated. I do the lyrics, musical direction, live drums, arrangement, mixing and mastering.
