An independent music project

Eigenstate Blue

Alternative-rock-influenced tracks built from lyrics, live drums, layered instruments and a slightly unlikely quantum-mechanics name.

About the project

Defined states, blurry emotions.

Eigenstate Blue is my artist name and music project, usually working in an alternative-rock style. In quantum mechanics, an eigenstate has a definite value; “blue” represents something more emotional and blurred in contrast.

I focus on the lyrics and instrumental side of the music. My own singing ability is truly laughable, so the vocal performances are not mine.

My workflow

I normally begin with lyrics, then build the instrumental in GarageBand—recording the drums live and constructing the remaining parts on my laptop. The final vocal track is produced with AI software using the lyrics, instrumental and detailed line-by-line direction.

The vocal performance is AI-generated. I therefore describe the tracks as partially AI-generated, while the lyrics, musical direction, live drums, arrangement, mixing and mastering are part of my own production process.
